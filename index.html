GigInsight Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Summary: GigInsight is designed to keep your gig-work information on your device. The current version does not require an account, does not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not send your gig-work records to the developer.

Information you enter or create

GigInsight lets you enter and store information such as gig-work earnings, tips, incentives, mileage, work-time states, order timing/history, vehicle operating-cost settings, tax-reserve settings, gig profiles, and other app preferences.

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the app's tracking, calculations, reports, and history.

Imports, exports, and backups

GigInsight can import supported files and can export or share backups or reports when you choose to do so. Imported data is processed for app functionality. When you export or share a file, Android's system file picker or share interface may let you choose another app or storage provider. GigInsight does not automatically send those files to the developer.

Data collection and sharing

The current version of GigInsight does not operate a developer-controlled cloud backend and does not collect or transmit your gig-work records to the developer. GigInsight does not sell or rent your personal information.

The current version does not use third-party advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or the Android Advertising ID.

Location

The current version of GigInsight does not automatically track GPS location or collect precise or approximate location data.

Notifications and foreground service

On Android, GigInsight uses notifications and a foreground service to support the user-started work/order timer and to keep timer status visible while the app is not in the foreground. This functionality does not, by itself, send your timer data to the developer.

Accounts and authentication

The current version does not require a GigInsight account or sign-in.

Data retention and deletion

Your app data remains on your device until you delete records in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Any backup files you create are controlled by you and remain wherever you choose to save or share them.

Security

Because GigInsight stores its working data locally, access to that data depends in part on the security of your device and any locations where you choose to save exported backups.

Children

GigInsight is intended for adult gig workers and is not directed to children under 18.

Future features and policy changes

If GigInsight later adds features such as cloud accounts, analytics, advertising, subscriptions, automatic GPS mileage tracking, or other data-processing features, this Privacy Policy and the applicable app-store disclosures will be updated before or when those features are released.

Contact

For privacy questions about GigInsight, use the developer support contact listed on the GigInsight Google Play store listing.
