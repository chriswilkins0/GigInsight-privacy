GigInsight Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Summary: GigInsight is designed to keep your gig-work information on your device. The app does not require an account, does not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not send your gig-work records to the developer. On Android, optional user-started automatic mileage tracking uses device location.

Information you enter or create

GigInsight lets you enter and store information such as gig-work earnings, tips, incentives, mileage, work-time states, order timing and history, vehicle operating-cost settings, tax-reserve settings, gig profiles, and other app preferences.

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the app's tracking, calculations, reports, and history.

Imports, exports, and backups

GigInsight can import supported files and can export or share backups or reports when you choose to do so. Imported data is processed for app functionality. When you export or share a file, your device's system file picker or share interface may let you choose another app or storage provider. GigInsight does not automatically send those files to the developer.

Data collection and sharing

The current version of GigInsight does not operate a developer-controlled cloud backend and does not collect or transmit your gig-work records to the developer. GigInsight does not sell or rent your personal information.

The current version does not use third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

Location

On supported Android versions, GigInsight can use precise device location to automatically measure driving distance while you have started a shift and mileage tracking is enabled. GPS location can continue to be used while GigInsight is in the background, another app is on screen, or the device is locked. Mileage tracking stops when the user-started shift ends. If you do not grant location permission, you can still use GigInsight's other features and enter mileage manually.

Driving distances, GPS-based mileage leg endpoints, saved places and the related history are maintained in GigInsight's local app storage. GigInsight does not upload these gig-work records or GPS history to a developer-controlled backend and does not use location for advertising or analytics. Android's platform location and reverse-geocoding providers may process coordinates when obtaining a GPS fix or resolving location coordinates to a readable address; their handling is subject to their own policies. If you export or share a backup or mileage report, the destination you select may receive information included in that export.

Notifications and background operation

Depending on platform and version, GigInsight may use operating-system notifications and a foreground service to support user-started timers, optional quick controls, and automatic mileage recording while a shift is active, even when the app is not on screen. This functionality does not, by itself, send your timer data to the developer.

Accounts and authentication

The current version does not require a GigInsight account or sign-in.

Data retention and deletion

Your app data remains on your device until you delete records in the app, clear the app's storage where supported, or uninstall the app. Any backup files you create are controlled by you and remain wherever you choose to save or share them.

Security

GigInsight keeps its working data in the app's local device storage rather than sending it to a developer-controlled server. Access to that data is protected by the security controls of your device. Exported backups or reports are stored only in locations you choose, so you should protect those files according to the sensitivity of the information they contain.

Children

GigInsight is intended for gig workers and is not directed to children.

Future features and policy changes

If GigInsight later adds features such as cloud accounts, analytics, advertising, subscriptions, or other new data-processing features, this Privacy Policy and applicable app-store disclosures will be updated as appropriate before or when those features are released.

Contact

For privacy questions about GigInsight, email support@giginsight.app or visit the GigInsight Support page.
