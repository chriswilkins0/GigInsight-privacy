GigInsight Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

Summary: GigInsight is designed to keep your gig-work information on your device. The app does not require an account, does not use advertising or general-purpose behavior analytics, and does not send your gig-work records to the developer. Google's Android ML Kit screenshot reader may send diagnostic and usage metrics to Google as described below. On supported Android and iOS releases, user-started automatic mileage tracking uses device location while a shift is active.

Information you enter or create

GigInsight lets you enter and store information such as gig-work earnings, tips, incentives, mileage, work-time states, order timing and history, vehicle operating-cost settings, tax-reserve settings, gig profiles, and other app preferences.

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to provide the app's tracking, calculations, reports, and history.

Imports, exports, and backups

GigInsight can import supported files and can export or share backups or reports when you choose to do so. Imported data is processed for app functionality. When you export or share a file, your device's system file picker or share interface may let you choose another app or storage provider. GigInsight does not automatically send those files to the developer.

Screenshot earnings import

If you explicitly select an earnings screenshot, GigInsight processes it on your device to identify and highlight possible monetary amounts. On supported iPhones, it uses Apple's system Photos picker and on-device Vision text recognition. On supported Android devices, it uses the system file or photo picker and on-device text recognition. You must review and confirm earnings before they are saved; no screenshot silently updates your records.

GigInsight may temporarily cache the selected image to display it during review. It stores confirmed earnings, a locally saved image fingerprint for duplicate prevention, and any gig-profile layout positions you choose to teach it. The original screenshot is not included in the saved earnings record and is not sent to the developer. You can re-learn your profile layout if a gig application's screen changes. The iPhone feature does not require permission to browse your full photo library.

Data collection and sharing

The current version of GigInsight does not operate a developer-controlled cloud backend and does not collect or transmit your gig-work records to the developer. GigInsight does not sell or rent your personal information.

GigInsight does not include advertising SDKs or general-purpose behavior analytics SDKs. The Android text-recognition component, Google ML Kit, can independently transmit limited technical diagnostics and API-usage metrics to Google as described below.

Android OCR diagnostic data (Google ML Kit)

GigInsight's Android earnings-screenshot feature uses Google's bundled ML Kit text-recognition SDK. The screenshot image, its detected text, and the recognition output are processed on the device and are not sent to Google by ML Kit.

Google states that ML Kit may send limited device and app information, per-installation identifiers, API-usage events, image-format and input/output size metadata, performance measurements, and error codes to Google's servers for diagnostics, maintenance, API usage analytics, and misuse prevention. This technical telemetry does not include your screenshot pixels, detected earnings amounts, or gig-work history. Google states that the ML Kit telemetry is encrypted in transit and is not shared with third parties. See Google's ML Kit Android data disclosure for details.

Location

On supported Android and iOS versions, GigInsight can use precise device location to automatically measure driving distance during a shift you start. GPS location can continue to be used while GigInsight is in the background, another app is on screen, or the device is locked. Mileage tracking stops when the user-started shift ends. If you do not grant location permission, you can still use GigInsight's other features and enter mileage manually.

Driving distances, GPS-based mileage leg endpoints, saved places and the related history are maintained in GigInsight's local app storage. GigInsight does not upload these gig-work records or GPS history to a developer-controlled backend and does not use location for advertising or analytics. Your phone's operating-system location and reverse-geocoding services (including Apple Core Location and geocoding on iOS, and Android location and geocoding providers) may process location information to provide GPS fixes or readable addresses under their respective policies. If you export or share a backup or mileage report, the destination you select may receive information included in that export.

Notifications and background operation

Depending on platform and version, GigInsight may use Android foreground services or iOS user-started background location updates, plus operating-system notifications and quick controls, to support timers and automatic mileage recording while a shift is active even when the app is not on screen. This functionality does not, by itself, send your timer data to the developer.

Accounts and authentication

The current version does not require a GigInsight account or sign-in.

Data retention and deletion

Your app data remains on your device until you delete records in the app, clear the app's storage where supported, or uninstall the app. Any backup files you create are controlled by you and remain wherever you choose to save or share them.

Security

GigInsight keeps its working data in the app's local device storage rather than sending it to a developer-controlled server. Access to that data is protected by the security controls of your device. Exported backups or reports are stored only in locations you choose, so you should protect those files according to the sensitivity of the information they contain.

Children

GigInsight is intended for gig workers and is not directed to children.

Future features and policy changes

If GigInsight later adds features such as cloud accounts, analytics, advertising, subscriptions, or other new data-processing features, this Privacy Policy and applicable app-store disclosures will be updated as appropriate before or when those features are released.
